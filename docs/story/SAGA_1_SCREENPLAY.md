**REALITIES OF DREAMS: MINECRAFT MULTIVERSE OF MADNESS**

**SAGA 1 --- THE FIRST STEVE**

MC-001 • Scenes 001--020 • Full playable narrative screenplay draft • Version 1

This is the beginning of Primordial Steve's journey, long before he understands the Multiverse, encounters Herobrine or learns about his eventual importance.

The opening should feel like a cinematic Minecraft survival adventure. Steve begins as an experienced, capable but ordinary resident of MC-001. He has friends, responsibilities and a home. Over twenty scenes, a seemingly local mystery draws him into the history of six civilisations and an ancient gateway that should not exist.

We will preserve the established twenty scene beats from our previous story registry. The more detailed Scene 001 proposal had combined some events that belong in Scenes 002--004; this version separates them into their proper order.

**Critical storyline restrictions**

Steve does not meet MysteryDweller, Professor Steve, Chrono Steve, Movie Steve or Cosmic Steve during this saga. We don't reveal the TMMA, the identities of the ancient Architects, the Root Command Block's true purpose, fictional Notch and Jeb, or the origin of the wider Multiverse.

The portal briefly activates in Scene 019 but is not a usable inter-universal travel route yet.

All dialogue and specific mission mechanics below are new screenplay proposals for your approval. The numbered events and established character identities come from our existing saga plan.

**ACT I --- THE DISTURBANCE**

Scenes 001--005 • Introduce Firstbrook, its people and the mystery

**Scene 001 --- A Morning in Firstbrook**

MC-001

Firstbrook • Early morning • 10--15 minutes

Playable characters: Primordial Steve NPCs: Elara, Elder Rowan, Orin, Bram, villagers Main quest: A New Morning

**Opening cinematic**

Black screen. The sound of flowing water fades in, followed by birds, footsteps and the distant strike of a blacksmith's hammer.

The camera moves slowly over Awakening Valley, revealing a vast landscape of mountains, forests, rivers and villages. It descends toward Firstbrook, a thriving Minecraft settlement.

Villagers are working, farmers are harvesting, and guards patrol the paths.

The camera enters Steve's modest house. Inside are his crafting table, furnace, armour stand, tools, stored resources and trophies from ordinary adventures.

Steve is asleep.

Sunlight reaches his bed.

He opens his eyes.

STEVE: "Another morning."

Steve rises, stretches and looks through the window.

Elara is passing outside.

ELARA: "Steve! You awake in there?"

STEVE: "I am now."

ELARA: "Orin said you'd sleep until noon if nobody checked."

STEVE: "Orin says that every morning."

ELARA: "And he's usually right."

Steve laughs quietly.

**Gameplay begins**

The camera moves behind Steve. The player takes control.

The UI introduces movement, jumping, inventory and the environment organically, without pausing for a mandatory tutorial screen.

The player can inspect Steve's home, equip basic equipment, collect breakfast from storage, and leave through the front door.

Quest objectives:

-   Leave Steve's house.

-   Explore Firstbrook.

-   Reach the village square.

There is no requirement to click Elara. She naturally walks beside Steve if the player follows the village path.

**Walking conversation**

ELARA: "You repaired the eastern bridge yesterday?"

STEVE: "Most of it. Bram helped with the supports."

ELARA: "You mean Bram watched while you did everything?"

STEVE: "He carried the wood."

ELARA: "That's something, I suppose."

They pass farmers and villagers.

FARMER: "Morning, Steve!"

STEVE: "Morning!"

ELARA: "I wish every morning was this quiet."

STEVE: "Don't say that. Something always happens when somebody says that."

Elara smiles.

**Environmental foreshadowing**

As Steve reaches the village square, the camera briefly frames a distant ridge.

A faint rumble is heard.

A hanging lantern sways.

A few birds suddenly leave the trees.

Most villagers don't notice.

STEVE: "Did you feel that?"

ELARA: "Feel what?"

Another barely perceptible vibration.

STEVE: "Probably nothing."

Elara turns toward the northern hills.

ELARA: "I hope so."

**Scene ending**

Elder Rowan appears at the opposite side of the square, visibly concerned.

He notices Steve.

ROWAN: "Steve! There you are."

The screen fades into regular gameplay as Rowan approaches.

Mission complete --- A New Morning

Rewards: Firstbrook journal entry; starter tools and supplies. Persistent consequences: Firstbrook exploration unlocked; Elara's companion relationship initialised. Next scene: Rowan's warning activates automatically when the player reaches conversational distance.

**Scene 002 --- The Warning from the North**

Firstbrook village square • Morning • 8--12 minutes

Characters: Steve, Rowan, Elara, Bram Quest: The Northern Disturbance

**Opening**

Rowan approaches Steve while the player remains in control.

ROWAN: "Steve, I've been looking everywhere for you."

STEVE: "What's wrong?"

ROWAN: "Something happened near the old northern ruins before sunrise."

ELARA: "The ruins? Nobody's been working there."

ROWAN: "Exactly. The ground shook, and the watchtower reported strange lights beneath the stone."

STEVE: "An earthquake?"

ROWAN: "I lived through earthquakes when I was younger. This felt different."

ELARA: "Different how?"

ROWAN: "Almost deliberate."

**Gameplay**

Steve must examine the aftermath of the disturbance.

The player can inspect three environmental clues:

-   A damaged stone wall near the village square.

-   A compass whose needle is behaving unusually.

-   A watchtower report about light on the northern ridge.

These are physical investigation actions, not conversations requiring clicks.

Bram arrives from his patrol.

BRAM: "The northern path isn't safe. Loose rocks came down just before dawn."

STEVE: "Anyone hurt?"

BRAM: "No. I closed the trail before the traders reached it."

ROWAN: "Good. I'd rather lose a morning of trading than a single life."

STEVE: "I'll have a look."

ROWAN: "Not alone. We need to know what we're dealing with."

**Cinematic**

The player raises the strange compass. Its needle spins.

The camera cuts briefly to the northern mountains.

A very faint light flickers beneath a distant ruin, then vanishes.

ELARA: "That's where I was heading."

STEVE: "Then let's find out what happened."

ROWAN: "First, prepare properly. There'll be no heroics today."

**Outcome**

The northern investigation is approved. Steve is appointed to lead the field survey, with Elara scouting and Orin supplying equipment.

Mission complete: Inspect all essential disturbances. Unlocked: Scene 003, northern trail reconnaissance.

Northern trail • Late morning • 12--18 minutes

Elara leads Steve to the northern edge of the valley.

ELARA: "Look at this."

ELARA: "I know this valley. I've crossed it since I was a child."

STEVE: "You make it sound like the valley betrayed you."

ELARA: "Maybe that's exactly how it feels."

The markings emit a quiet pulse when Steve gets close.

STEVE: "Did you see that?"

STEVE: "They reacted to me."

ELARA: "Or to the equipment you're carrying. We don't know yet."

**Scene 004 --- The Craftsman's Warning**

Orin's workshop • Midday • 10--15 minutes

Characters: Steve, Orin, Elara Quest: Tools for the Unknown

**Opening**

Orin is repairing damaged tools when Steve enters his workshop.

He notices Steve automatically.

ORIN: "Before you ask, no, I haven't worked out what happened."

STEVE: "We haven't asked yet."

ORIN: "Your faces asked for you."

ELARA: "We found markings near the northern path."

ORIN: "Markings?"

Elara places a sketch on the workbench.

Orin stops working.

ORIN: "Where did you find that?"

STEVE: "On a rock near the ridge."

ORIN: "That's not a natural crack."

**Gameplay**

The player assembles a survey kit at the workbench.

Components include a compass, rope, chalk, torches and a reinforced pickaxe. Rather than clicking Orin to complete a quest, the player physically crafts and collects the required kit.

ORIN: "You can break most stone with a good pickaxe."

STEVE: "That's usually the idea."

ORIN: "This time, don't."

ELARA: "He's serious, Steve."

ORIN: "Very serious. I want measurements before somebody destroys the evidence."

**Cinematic**

Orin holds the compass near Elara's sketch.

The needle spins toward the paper.

A metallic vibration fills the workshop.

STEVE: "That's not normal."

ORIN: "Glad we've established that."

**Outcome**

The survey kit is complete. Orin joins the investigation as a technical adviser from Firstbrook, and his workshop becomes the player's equipment support point.

Gameplay unlocks: Crafting-based investigation tools, map annotations and repair station.

**Scene 005 --- Bram's Volunteers**

Firstbrook north gate • Afternoon • 12--18 minutes

Characters: Steve, Bram, Elara, Orin, Rowan, village guards Quest: Secure the Northern Road

**Opening cinematic**

Bram is assembling a small volunteer team outside the gate.

BRAM: "Everyone listen! Nobody goes past the north marker without an escort."

One villager objects.

VOLUNTEER: "It's just an old ruin."

BRAM: "Then you won't mind waiting until we know it's safe."

Steve arrives.

BRAM: "Good. I need somebody who knows how to build a proper barricade."

STEVE: "You could've just asked."

BRAM: "I was hoping you'd volunteer."

**Playable construction and defence**

The player helps Bram prepare the northern road.

Objectives involve placing protective barriers, repairing a watch platform, lighting a safe route and moving supplies.

A minor environmental hazard tests the player's reactions: falling stone damages part of the path, threatening a nearby worker.

Steve must reach the worker, clear the obstruction and restore a safe crossing.

WORKER: "Help! I can't get through!"

STEVE: "Stay back from the rocks!"

BRAM: "I'll clear the other side!"

After the rescue:

BRAM: "Nicely done."

STEVE: "We're lucky nobody got hurt."

BRAM: "Luck won't keep us safe forever."

**Final cinematic**

From the restored watch platform, Steve spots the silhouette of an ancient monument beyond the valley.

ELARA: "There it is."

STEVE: "The ruins?"

ORIN: "Not exactly. That structure doesn't match any record I've seen."

ROWAN: "Then we proceed carefully."

STEVE: "We go tomorrow."

The camera rises above the valley, showing the monument against the evening sky.

**Outcome**

The north road is secure. The investigation party can safely enter the ancient ruins.

Act I complete: Firstbrook and its characters are established, the player understands basic survival and investigation mechanics, and the mystery begins to feel consequential.

**ACT II --- THE ANCIENT MONUMENT**

Scenes 006--010 • Ancient technology, exploration and the first warning from another civilisation

**Scene 006 --- The Fractured Monument**

Ancient northern monument • Following morning • 15--20 minutes

Characters: Primordial Steve, Elara, Bram, Orin Quest: The Stone That Remembers

**Opening cinematic**

The party walks along a narrow mountain trail.

In the distance stands an enormous ancient monument, partially covered by vines and centuries of weathering.

Its central pillar has split open, revealing unfamiliar geometric patterns.

The camera follows Steve and the others approaching the structure.

BRAM: "That's bigger than it looked from the watchtower."

ELARA: "The outer walls weren't visible yesterday."

ORIN: "They've been buried for generations. The disturbance must've exposed them."

STEVE: "Then the ground didn't just shake. Something underneath moved."

ORIN: "That's what worries me."

**Quest gameplay**

The player explores the monument's surrounding courtyard.

The site contains three investigative challenges:

1.  Collapsed entrance: Use blocks to construct a stable staircase, providing access without destroying the monument.

2.  Stone carvings: Examine three panels to record unfamiliar symbols.

3.  Central pillar: Use Orin's measuring equipment to inspect the fracture.

Elara searches the upper terraces independently, while Bram checks the courtyard for dangers.

**Environmental discovery**

The player uncovers a carved image depicting six separate structures connected around a central circular symbol.

STEVE: "Six towers?"

ORIN: "Possibly. Or six settlements."

ELARA: "Look at their shapes. That one resembles the towers of Eldervale."

BRAM: "And that one looks like the old Stonecrown fortress."

STEVE: "Why would somebody carve all six here?"

Orin studies the carving.

ORIN: "I don't know. But somebody wanted them remembered together."

**Cinematic event**

Steve places his hand near the fractured central pillar while examining it.

A faint light moves through its carved lines.

The surrounding ground vibrates.

Elara quickly steps toward him.

ELARA: "Steve! Move away!"

Steve steps back.

The light fades.

STEVE: "I didn't touch anything."

ORIN: "I saw. It started before your hand reached the stone."

BRAM: "Then we're not staying here after dark."

**Outcome**

The party successfully records the monument's markings. Orin collects a harmless fallen stone fragment for analysis.

New discovery: Six connected civilisation symbols.

Persistent changes: The monument appears on the player's map; excavation logs and collected symbols are saved.

Next scene: Orin investigates the strange compass response.

**Scene 007 --- The Compass That Points Nowhere**

Northern monument and Firstbrook workshop • Morning • 12--18 minutes

Characters: Steve, Orin, Elara Quest: A Direction Without North

**Opening**

Steve returns to Orin's field table, where several compasses lie scattered.

Orin is frustrated.

ORIN: "I've tried three compasses. Every single one is wrong."

STEVE: "Maybe they're all broken."

ORIN: "All three? At exactly the same time?"

ELARA: "Orin, what are they pointing toward?"

Orin places them on the table.

Every needle turns toward the northern monument.

ORIN: "Whatever is buried beneath that structure."

**Gameplay --- Compass investigation**

The player receives an experimental compass.

It has two modes: ordinary navigation and anomalous signal detection.

Steve must walk between several survey points. The compass responds differently at each position.

Players must establish three readings and compare them on the survey map.

During the investigation, the compass temporarily points downward rather than toward the horizon.

STEVE: "The signal isn't ahead of us."

ORIN: "Then where?"

STEVE: "Below."

ELARA: "Below the monument?"

STEVE: "Farther than that, I think."

**Cinematic discovery**

Steve places the compass on a stone pedestal.

For three seconds, its needle spins before suddenly stopping.

A faint six-pointed shape appears above the compass.

Orin reaches toward it, but the projection disappears.

ORIN: "Did you both see that?"

ELARA: "Six points."

STEVE: "Like the monument carving."

ORIN: "We're going to need information from outside Firstbrook."

ELARA: "Maybe the other kingdoms have seen these symbols."

**Outcome**

The party identifies a possible route through Awakening Valley, leading to a second ancient survey location.

Gameplay unlock: Anomaly compass.

Next scene: Cross Awakening Valley and trace the signal.

**Scene 008 --- Across Awakening Valley**

Awakening Valley • Afternoon • 20--30 minutes

Characters: Steve, Elara, Bram Quest: Into the Valley



**Cinematic**

The camera rises over Awakening Valley.

A vast river cuts through the terrain, surrounded by steep cliffs, ancient bridges and forested hills.

Steve, Elara and Bram stand at the valley's edge.

ELARA: "Beautiful, isn't it?"

-   Locate three survey markers using the anomaly compass.

-   Collect provisions for the return journey.

-   Reach the eastern valley overlook.

Steve controls the party's route. Elara can scout ahead; Bram carries supplies and provides defence.

**Walking conversations**

ELARA: "Have you ever wondered what's beyond the kingdoms?"

BRAM: "Rowan said you were always getting into trouble as a child."

**Environmental challenge**

A section of the old bridge collapses under the weight of loose stone.

The player must rescue a supply crate before it falls into the river.

Bram assists by holding a rope while Elara identifies a route across.

This is a controlled navigation challenge, not a scripted failure.

**Closing cinematic**

At sunset, the compass needle swings toward a distant abandoned campsite.

ELARA: "Someone's been here."

BRAM: "Recently?"

STEVE: "Let's find out."

**Outcome**

The player establishes an expedition camp that can be revisited and used as a checkpoint.

Next scene: Investigate the deserted campsite.

**Scene 009 --- The Empty Campsite**

Eastern Awakening Valley • Sunset • 15--25 minutes

Characters: Steve, Elara, Bram Quest: The Missing Expedition

**Opening**

The party finds tents, extinguished campfires and scattered equipment.

There are no people.

The environment should suggest abandonment without relying on graphic horror or frightening creatures.

BRAM: "Nobody here."

ELARA: "The fire's cold, but these supplies haven't been here long."

STEVE: "They left in a hurry."

BRAM: "Or somebody made them leave."

**Investigation gameplay**

The campsite contains multiple clues:

-   A torn expedition journal.

-   A damaged lantern near the cliff edge.

-   A map with one section erased.

-   A set of tracks leading toward the forest.

-   An untouched supply chest.

The player needs to identify enough evidence to reconstruct what happened.

There are two optional routes: following the tracks or searching the expedition tents thoroughly.

**Dialogue at the journal**

ELARA: "These people were mapping the same markings we found."

STEVE: "How do you know?"

ELARA: "Look at this drawing."

She points to a sketch resembling the six-point symbol.

BRAM: "Who sent them?"

STEVE: "The last page is missing."

ELARA: "Maybe they carried it with them."

**Environmental cinematic**

The ground emits a low vibration.

A camp lantern rolls slightly across the table despite the absence of wind.

The anomaly compass reacts.

BRAM: "There it is again."

STEVE: "The same signal."

ELARA: "And it's getting stronger."

The player follows the compass to a weathered stone post at the camp's perimeter.

Carved into it is a woodland emblem.

ELARA: "Sylvaris."

STEVE: "The forest civilisation?"

ELARA: "Someone from Sylvaris knows about this site."

**Outcome**

The missing expedition becomes a recurring side mystery rather than an instant conclusion. Their fate remains unresolved.

Unlocks: Expedition journal entries and the optional Find the Missing Explorers questline.

Next scene: A warning arrives from Sylvaris.

**Scene 010 --- Liora's Warning**

Sylvaris border outpost • Following morning • 15--20 minutes

Characters: Steve, Elara, Bram, Liora, Sylvaris rangers Quest: The Forest's Message


Civilisation introduced: Sylvaris

The forest civilisation is part of MC-001. Liora is a female woodland representative with an independent role in protecting Sylvaris territory.

**Opening cinematic**

The player reaches a Sylvaris border outpost surrounded by enormous trees and elevated wooden platforms.

Two rangers observe Steve's party from above.

Liora steps onto the main platform.

LIORA: "Travellers from Firstbrook?"

ELARA: "Liora?"

LIORA: "Elara. I hoped the next time we met would be under better circumstances."

STEVE: "You've been expecting us?"

LIORA: "We've been expecting someone to follow the tremors."

**Dialogue sequence**

Liora explains that unusual vibrations have spread through the forest.

LIORA: "The oldest trees have stood here for generations. Their roots reach farther than our records."

STEVE: "And?"

LIORA: "Yesterday, the ground beneath them started humming."

BRAM: "Humming?"

LIORA: "A sound without a source. Some of our watchtowers heard it at the same time."

ELARA: "Did you find anything?"

LIORA: "An ancient stone buried beneath the eastern grove. It has the same markings as the drawing you brought."

Steve shows Liora the expedition journal.

LIORA: "Where did you get that?"

STEVE: "An abandoned camp."

LIORA: "Then those explorers may have reached farther than we realised."

**Playable forest investigation**

The player accompanies Liora to the eastern grove.

Objectives:

-   Follow the rangers without damaging protected forest areas.

-   Stabilise a fallen walkway.

-   Examine the exposed ancient stone.

-   Help Liora retrieve a historical record from a watchtower.

The player can choose between several routes, but the investigation outcome remains consistent.

**Cinematic discovery**

Liora compares the ancient stone with Steve's symbol.

LIORA: "Six marks. Six civilisations."

STEVE: "That's what we thought."

LIORA: "Then this is no longer a matter for Firstbrook alone."

ELARA: "What are you suggesting?"

LIORA: "You need the rulers and representatives together before someone makes a terrible mistake."

**Ending**

Liora prepares an official warning for Queen Aveline of Eldervale.

LIORA: "Take this to your elders. Ask them to contact the royal court."

STEVE: "Will Sylvaris help?"

LIORA: "I'll come myself."

STEVE: "Then we'll see you there."

**Outcome**

Liora becomes an important recurring ally and can later be selected as a companion for forest-related missions.

The mystery now concerns multiple MC-001 civilisations.

Act II complete.

Scenes 011--017 • Action, diplomacy, ancient history and conflicting claims

**Scene 011 --- The Raiders of the Eastern Road**

Eldervale trade road • Midday • 20--25 minutes

Characters: Steve, Elara, Bram, Liora, travelling merchants, raider leader Quest: Defend the Caravan

**Opening cinematic**

Steve's group escorts Liora's message toward Eldervale.

A merchant caravan travels alongside them.

MERCHANT: "You came from Sylvaris?"

LIORA: "The eastern outpost."

MERCHANT: "Then you may know why the roads have been so strange lately."

STEVE: "Strange how?"

MERCHANT: "People disappearing. Animals refusing to cross certain bridges. And raiders taking advantage of it."

Bram looks toward the hills.

BRAM: "Speaking of raiders..."

Several hostile figures emerge from the rocks and block the road.

RAIDER LEADER: "Leave the supplies and walk away!"

BRAM: "Wrong caravan."

STEVE: "Nobody needs to get hurt. Let the travellers pass."

RAIDER LEADER: "You've got a lot of confidence for someone holding a pickaxe."

STEVE: "It's a very good pickaxe."

**Combat gameplay**

This is the first substantial combat encounter.

The player must protect civilians, defeat or drive away raiders, and prevent the supply wagon from being destroyed.

Bram uses a shield and melee attacks. Elara engages enemies from the flanks. Liora protects civilians and provides ranged support.

Combat dialogue:

BRAM: "Steve! Keep them away from the wagon!"

ELARA: "Two coming from the ridge!"

STEVE: "I've got them!"

LIORA: "The merchants are moving! Hold the road!"

The battle features three escalating phases: a front assault, a flanking attempt, and the leader's final attack.

The player can use terrain, placed barricades and ordinary Minecraft combat mechanics. The outcome shouldn't depend on a single timed button press.

**Post-combat cinematic**

The raider leader drops his weapon and retreats.

RAIDER LEADER: "This road isn't worth dying for!"

The remaining raiders withdraw.

Bram approaches Steve.

BRAM: "Not bad."

STEVE: "You sound surprised."

BRAM: "I'm always surprised when your plans work."

Liora checks on the merchants.

LIORA: "Everyone's safe."

STEVE: "Then let's get them home."

**Hidden clue**

The caravan contains a sealed crate bearing a faded version of the six-point emblem.

A merchant claims it came from a ruined trading post and was being delivered to Eldervale.

**Outcome**

Eldervale's officials learn that Steve protected the caravan, improving his standing at court.

Rewards: Supplies, caravan gratitude, optional trade discounts, and a historical clue.

**Scene 012 --- The Queen's Summons**

Valebridge, Eldervale capital • Afternoon • 20--30 minutes

Characters: Steve, Elara, Bram, Liora, Queen Aveline, royal guards, advisers Quest: The Sixfold Council



**Opening cinematic**

The camera sweeps across Valebridge, the capital of Eldervale.

Its streets contain merchants, craftsmen, guards, residents and visiting representatives from neighbouring civilisations.

Steve and his companions enter through the city gates.

BRAM: "I've never seen so many people in one place."

ELARA: "Try not to stare."

BRAM: "I'm looking for the best place to eat."

STEVE: "Some things never change."

**Playable city sequence**

Rather than immediately teleporting the player into a throne room, the city is explorable.

Steve must deliver the investigation dossier to the royal archive. After the dossier is submitted, a messenger invites the group to a council meeting.

The player can optionally visit merchants, repair equipment, inspect maps and overhear NPC conversations.

**Council cinematic**

The throne room is spacious and illuminated by hanging lanterns.

Queen Aveline sits at the centre of the council chamber, surrounded by advisers.

Steve enters with his companions.

QUEEN AVELINE: "Primordial Steve of Firstbrook."

STEVE: "Your Majesty."

QUEEN AVELINE: "Rise. I've heard about the caravan you protected."

STEVE: "Anyone would've done the same."

QUEEN AVELINE: "Not everyone does."

She gestures toward the investigation materials.

QUEEN AVELINE: "Now tell me why six ancient symbols have brought you to my court."

Steve recounts what his group discovered.

STEVE: "Something beneath the northern ruins is active. The symbols appear in several locations, and the compass reacts to all of them."

QUEEN AVELINE: "And you believe those symbols correspond to our civilisations?"

LIORA: "We have found matching stonework in Sylvaris."

ELARA: "And evidence of an expedition that vanished while studying it."

The Queen turns to her advisers.

QUEEN AVELINE: "Then the remaining leaders must be informed."

**Dialogue choice**

The player chooses how to frame Steve's concern:

-   Cautious: "We don't understand this discovery. We need to investigate safely."

-   Urgent: "People may already be in danger. We cannot afford to wait."

-   Cooperative: "All six civilisations need to investigate this together."

The choice affects responses and relationships, not the fixed story outcome.

Aveline agrees to summon representatives.

QUEEN AVELINE: "The past belongs to all of us. Whatever was buried beneath these lands may affect all of us too."

STEVE: "Then we'll find the truth together."

**Outcome**

Steve receives permission to continue the investigation and access relevant royal archives.

Queen Aveline enters the main recurring cast. The wider MC-001 political landscape is introduced.

**Scene 013 --- The Stone of Irondeep**

Valebridge council chamber and archive • Following morning • 15--20 minutes

Characters: Steve, Queen Aveline, Thane Garrick, Orin, Elara Quest: The Engraved Stone

**Opening cinematic**

Heavy footsteps echo through the council chamber.

Thane Garrick enters with two Stonecrown guards carrying a stone case.

THANE GARRICK: "I was told you found something worth dragging me away from Irondeep."

QUEEN AVELINE: "You may decide for yourself."

**Cinematics, audio and technical requirements**

Cinematics

Camera tracking, establishing shots, natural transitions and character animation. Players keep movement control during ordinary conversations; major cinematics are skippable without losing quest progress.

Dialogue and sound

Every spoken line has a speaker ID, subtitle, timing and future voice-asset reference. Environmental sounds, footsteps, crafting, combat and gateway machinery require spatial audio.

Gameplay

Actual Minecraft survival mechanics, building, investigation tools, companion AI, one major raider encounter and recoverable environmental challenges. Scenes cannot be completed merely by watching cutscenes.

Multiplayer

Server-authoritative progression, individual quest states, opt-in party synchronisation, safe companion ownership and cinematic triggers that don't automatically interrupt unrelated players.

The playable saga needs the existing main character skins, appropriate NPC rigs and animations, a fully built Firstbrook, Awakening Valley, the monument, Sylvaris outpost, Valebridge, harbour, six-civilisation environmental details, ancient archaeological props and the gateway chamber.

We must also produce animations and voice recordings for the scripted conversations, soundtrack cues, quest items, combat behaviour, HUD updates and save checkpoints. Those assets are required, not yet verified as completed.
